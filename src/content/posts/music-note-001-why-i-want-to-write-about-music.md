Music is one of the fastest ways to remember a period of life. A song can hold a room, a season, a person, a mood, or a version of myself that I cannot explain directly.

I want to write about music because taste changes, and I want to notice how it changes. What do I hear now that I missed before? Which sounds stay with me? Which songs become attached to memory?

These notes do not need to be technical at first. They can start with listening: rhythm, mood, texture, lyrics, silence, and the feeling of time moving through sound.
