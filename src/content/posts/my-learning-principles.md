## Begin with not knowing

Knowing that I do not know is not weakness. It is the beginning of real study. If I start with certainty, I stop looking. If I start with ignorance, I can pay attention.

## Write before I feel ready

If I wait until I fully understand something, I will never write. Writing is how I find the gaps. A rough note is not the final answer. It is a tool for seeing what is missing.

## Separate signal from ego

A note is useful if it makes my thinking clearer, not if it makes me look smart. The point is not to perform confidence. The point is to reduce confusion.

## Keep a record of wrongness

Old mistakes are not embarrassing if I review them. They are embarrassing only if I repeat them blindly. A good archive should make old thinking visible enough to correct.

## Evidence first, opinion second

In markets, engineering, and life, opinions are cheap. Evidence is the discipline. I want to practice saying what I know, what I infer, and what I may be imagining.

## Build small things

Small projects expose fake understanding faster than passive reading. If I cannot make something work, I probably do not understand it as well as I thought.

## Review

A thought that is never reviewed is not a system. It is just a mood. I want this site to make revision easier than forgetting.
