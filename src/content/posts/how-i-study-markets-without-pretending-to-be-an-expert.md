This is a personal learning journal, not financial advice. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any investment product.

## Why markets interest me

Markets interest me because they combine business, psychology, incentives, time, and uncertainty. A company is not only a ticker. It is a system of customers, costs, capital, management choices, and expectations.

## Why I need humility

It is easy to repeat confident language without understanding the evidence underneath it. My current goal is not to sound certain. My goal is to notice where my understanding is thin.

## My template for studying a company

When I study a company, I want to write down the topic, the company or industry, why I am studying it, what I think I understand, the evidence, what I might be wrong about, key risks, what would change my mind, decision or status, review date, and lessons.

## Why risk matters more than excitement

Excitement is cheap. Risk is where the work begins. The risk I may be underestimating is often more important than the upside I can describe easily.

## How I want to review my old thoughts

I want each markets note to become a future review object. If my thinking changes, I should be able to see why. This is a note to myself, not a public recommendation.
