I was born not knowing. I am still learning.

This site is a public archive of what I study: engineering, English, markets, music, and the process of thinking clearer.

I do not want to pretend to be an expert. I want to record the work before the results.

If my thinking changes, I will update the notes. If I am wrong, I will write the post-mortem.

This is not a personal brand project. This is a learning ledger.
