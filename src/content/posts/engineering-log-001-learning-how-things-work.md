Engineering attracts me because it forces ideas to meet constraints. A system either works under real conditions or it does not. That honesty is useful.

I want to understand how physical things work: automation, electronics, signals, materials, power, sensors, feedback, and the chain of cause and effect. Reading gives me language, but building and testing reveal the gaps.

The important habit is to slow down. What is the input? What changes state? What is measured? What fails first? What assumption did I make without noticing?

This log starts as a place for fundamentals. Not polished expertise. Just a record of learning the shape of systems.
