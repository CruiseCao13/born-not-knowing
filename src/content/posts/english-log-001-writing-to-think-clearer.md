I want to improve my English because language is not only a skill for communication. It is also a tool for thinking.

When a sentence is vague, the thought behind it is often vague too. When I try to translate an idea clearly, I notice where the idea is unfinished.

This log is for words I misuse, sentences I want to remember, short translations, speaking notes, and essays that help me express thought with less fog.

The goal is not fancy English. The goal is honest English: simple, accurate, and alive enough to carry what I mean.
